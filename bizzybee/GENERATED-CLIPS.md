Two separate problems.

**Resolution.** 320px on the long edge needs a 3.4× upscale to reach 1080×1920.
It will look soft next to the generated 720p footage, and cutting between the two
will show the difference.

**Brand.** The discoloured water is the visual centrepiece of clips 1 and 3, and
in a *relaxing spa* ad it works against the brief twice over: it is not a calm,
clean, wellness image, and it silently makes exactly the toxin-release claim the
ad is supposed to avoid. Saying nothing while showing brown water does not
actually avoid the claim.
